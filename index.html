Practice

A focused practice in employment-based immigration, where the quality of the legal argument and the evidence decides the result.

  • Form I-140

    EB-1A

    Extraordinary ability. A structured evidentiary case for sustained acclaim in the field.

  • Form I-140

    EB-2 NIW

    National Interest Waiver. Self-petition without a job offer, built on the merit of the endeavor.

  • Form I-129

    O-1A

    Temporary status for individuals of extraordinary ability who need to work in the United States.

  • I-140 & I-129

    EB-1B, EB-1C, L-1

    Outstanding researchers, multinational managers and intracompany transferees.

  • Post-filing
