## About

Bhimesh Singh prepares U.S. employment-based immigration petitions end to end: from the first prospect call and client intake, through forms, legal drafting and evidence compilation, to final filing. Petition categories include EB-1A, EB-2 NIW, O-1A, EB-1B, EB-1C and L-1, along with RFE, NOID and AAO appeal matters.

He began in Indian litigation, drafting pleadings, affidavits and legal notices in the Surat District and Sessions Court, and later led final legal and quality review of petitions as a Team Lead at a U.S. immigration law firm. He also mentors lawyers and aspiring legal professionals on building international legal careers.
