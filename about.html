Bhimesh Singh is a lawyer focused on U.S. employment-based immigration, with a record of preparing and filing petitions on tight timelines.

Bhimesh Singh · Advocate, BCI

Immigration cases are won in the drafting.

I am an Advocate enrolled with the Bar Council of India, and my daily work is U.S. employment-based immigration: EB-1A, EB-2 NIW, O-1A, EB-1B, EB-1C and L-1 petitions. I prepare each case from the first prospect call through client intake, forms, legal drafting and evidence compilation, to final submission.

I began in Indian litigation, drafting pleadings, affidavits and legal notices in the Surat District and Sessions Court. That grounding in precise written argument is what I bring to petitions today. In immigration, as in court, a reviewing officer reads closely, and the case has to answer every question before it is asked.

As a Team Lead I performed the final legal and quality review of petitions before filing, assessing legal sufficiency, evidentiary strength and alignment with current USCIS practice and AAO decisions. I also handled escalated cases, denial reviews and motion and appeal assessments.

I mentor lawyers and aspiring legal professionals on building international legal careers, and share practical insight on immigration law through my content.
